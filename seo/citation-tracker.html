Keep the listings working

Five rules

  1. Same details, everywhere"iContact Africa Solutions", "1605 Crowborough North, Harare" and +263 71 857 4277, exactly. No "Pvt Ltd" on some and not others.
  2. One website linkEvery listing points to the GitHub Pages address below. If you move to your own domain later, update every Live listing; the Link fields here make that a 30-minute job.
  3. Verify, don't just submitMany directories (Google, Bing, Apple, Clutch) only show a listing after a phone, email or postcard check. Keep the 071 phone and your Gmail nearby.
  4. Ask for reviewsGoogle, Clutch, GoodFirms and Trustpilot rank you by reviews. Ask MC Attorneys, Parker Paints and other happy clients for one each.
  5. Never pay for "1,000 directory submissions"Spammy bulk listings can hurt rankings. The list above is enough.
