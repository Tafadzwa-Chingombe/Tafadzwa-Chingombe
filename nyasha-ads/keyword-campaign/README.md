3. Then plan for yearly costs: your .co.zw domain, hosting, and a business email.
4. At iContact, there's no inbox for price. Tell us what you need, and we'll send you a fixed quote.
5. Book your free call. Tatenda!

### 5 signs you are overpaying for your website
1. Mhoro! Five signs you're paying too much for your website.
2. One: the domain isn't in your name. Two: surprise renewal fees. Three: no written quote.
3. Four: you pay for every small update. Five: it's slow on mobile data.
4. With us, the domain is yours, the price is fixed, and it's all in writing.
5. Book a free call for a second opinion. Zvakanaka!

### A Facebook page is not a website
1. Ndine page yeFacebook, handidi website. Sound familiar?
2. A Facebook page is rented space. A website is yours: it shows up on Google, takes EcoCash and Visa payments, and no algorithm decides who sees it.
3. Keep your page for conversations, and send people to a website that closes the sale.
4. Book a free call. Zvakanaka!

### Website + WhatsApp, working together
1. Mhoro! Should you sell on WhatsApp, or on a website? The answer is both.
2. Customers find you on Google, see your prices on your website, tap to order on WhatsApp, and pay with EcoCash.
3. Your website answers the questions. WhatsApp closes the deal.
4. Let's set it up. Book a free call. Tatenda!

### How to pay for Facebook ads from Zimbabwe
1. Boost ndoibhadhara sei? Card declined when you try to boost a post?
2. Meta often declines local cards, and it doesn't accept ZiG.
3. The fix: get a USD prepaid Visa card from your bank, add it under Billing and payments, load it before you boost, and set a daily budget.
4. Or let us run your ads for you. Book a free call. Zvakanaka!

### Get your business on Google Maps
1. Mhoro! Can customers find your business on Google Maps? Here's how, in four steps.
2. Go to Google Business Profile, add your name, category and location, verify your business, then add photos, opening hours and your phone number.
3. Now, when someone nearby searches, you show up, with directions and a call button.
4. Want it done for you? Book a free call.

### Is your website POTRAZ-ready?
1. Mhoro! Is your website ready for Zimbabwe's data protection rules?
2. If your contact form or customer list collects names, phone numbers or emails, you may need a data controller licence from POTRAZ.
3. POTRAZ began compliance inspections in September 2026. Start with a privacy policy, consent on your forms, a secure site, and knowing where your data is stored.
4. Book a free call, and check the legal details with a lawyer.

### Agency vs freelancer vs DIY
1. Mhoro! Agency, freelancer, or build it yourself?
2. DIY is cheapest, but it costs your time. A freelancer can be great, just get a contract. An agency gives you a team and aftercare.
3. Whoever you choose, ask three things: who owns the domain, is the price in writing, and who fixes it after launch?
4. Ask us anything. Book a free call. Tatenda!
