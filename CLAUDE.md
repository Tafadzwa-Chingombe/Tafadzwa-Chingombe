# Project instructions

## Ad copy and voice-overs
- **English only in all ad copy and voice-overs.** No Shona, Ndebele or other local-language words or phrases in
  voice-over scripts, captions, on-screen text, headlines, CTAs or social captions — including greetings and
  sign-offs (use "Hi!", "Thank you!", not *Mhoro*, *Tatenda*, *Zvakanaka*). Translate any local-language lines a brief
  or research doc supplies into plain English, and say so.
- **Contact details: phone numbers, not the website.** Every new ad, video or piece of ad copy shows
  "Call or WhatsApp +263 71 857 4277 · +263 77 419 2027" instead of icontactafricasolutions.org (on screen and in
  voice-overs). Existing videos made before November 2026 are left as they are unless asked.
- Nyasha video ads are made with the `nyasha-video-ads` skill (`.claude/skills/nyasha-video-ads/SKILL.md`).
